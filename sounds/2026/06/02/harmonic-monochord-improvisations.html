Harmonic Monochord Improvisations

Jun 2, 2026

 

During the year 2025 I recorded a few improvisations. These are mostly uncut.

The widget appearing on the right side of the screen will provide an approximative visualisation of the harmonic content of the recordings mapped onto an harmonic spiral.

 

I

Notes:

Harmonic series / Opening chord and first ringing pattern.

7’ open change ringing.

9’ going down.

10’ upward harmonic waves.

14’ first natural resonance.

15’ new high pitch space is reached. Exploration. Free curves.

17’ straight line, transition

18’ slow dissolution while going up in frequency.

 

II



Notes:

f0 root + slow melody

4’ moving the base tone 2 octaves higher

5’ slow chord

6’30 mixing harmonics + change ringing

7’ free exploration

9’ finger contact / abrupt interruption of the vibration, glass effect with a triad.

11’45 octave beatings

13’ hf ripples

 

III



Notes:

Octave intro, beatings, base tone slowly fades out

4’ base in the mid-range: long and sustained tones with some high-pitch shimmering. Not a melodic but a textural / single chord elaboration. The high-pitch part gets sometimes a breath-like quality.

6’ Zooming-in on a really quiet pattern. Rays occasionally appear.

9’ transition tone, recentering.

11’ low chord, growth and deployment of higher harmonics, quiet but steady radiation.

12’ fading gradually into the upper register and pure shimmering. A bit of change ringing.

 

IV



Notes:

Intro with parallel octaves, light touches

3’30 first dramatic finger contact.

4’30 going higher.

5’ high pitch chord. Occasional decelerations.

6’ straight note, transition.

8’ vibrating chord, change ringing.

9’ going down the harmonic scale and end.

 

V



Notes:

Shepherd’s bell intro // footsteps in the snow.

3’ slow exploration.

5’ downward arpeggio + finger opening.

6’ nice expansion, transition with a straight tone.

8’ continuous tone, circular effect.

9’ fingerwork + straight tone / octave.

11’ high pitch bubbles.

12 open calls.

14’ a chord emerges and resolves itself.

16’ spiral, mixed movements and straight finishing line.

 

VI



Notes:

Long base tone + natural cyclical ringing in the upper register.

3’ playing and resume the vibration.

4’30 finger, space-opening + nice change ringing.

6’ high sphere exploration. A light pattern slowly shows itself. A low tone slowly enters and help the whole structure.

7’ a high pitch pattern keeps unveiling itself. Remanence.

9’ really good narration.

10’ a very high-pitch pattern isolate itself.

12’ micro-work on 4 harmonics.

 

VII



Notes:

Playing with the fingertip on the string to “launch” harmonics.

 

VIII



Notes:

Trying to maintain two parallel streams. The lower one alternates slowly, the higher one is here as a side-reflection, ondulating in a faster way.

4’30 - Transitioning through a continuous tone into a shimmering acoustic space.

5’ - Some harmonic tension is presented.

6’ - A few disharmonic moves are made.

7’ - Aborted upward movement.

 

X



Upward/Downard contrary motion opening Figure.

4’ - Free moves as a warm-up, light tapping.

6’ - Dissonant shape exploration. Sructural tones appear as rays. They “carry” the shape.

7’ - first apparition of high frequencies.

7’50 - transition to very high frequencies through a single threaded lightbeam.

8’ - new high pitched space is opened. Slight movement.

9’ - falling in a state of weightlessness.

10’ - the high pitch sphere is still there.

11’30 - a stronger low frequency is growing.

12’ - detuning while playing.

14’ - energy is dissolving, evaporating / transition tone.

15’ - natural harmonics, sudden upward move, feeling of urgency, fall.

17’ - play with interacting harmonic pair.

19’ - light natural harmonic. End of the process, pace is slowing down. Dissolution in the high pitch range.

XI



Dyad with light reflexions.

2’ High zone is partly taking over, attracting lower rays towards it. Rotation, pulsation, radiating vibrations.

4’ Transition with braided rays (3 smaller, thinner intertwined rays) . Moving into the harmonic space.

6’ Calmer waves trying to raise.

9’ Finally a higher vibration emerges and sticks.

11’ The shape still tries to radiate. It mostly flickers. Reminisences of higher tones sometimes appear.

13’ Change ringing.

14’ Resolution and end.

14’ Contrary movement. Going up the scale but very slowly with tap interruptions.

18’ High pitch exploration. Some sparse very high pitch tones give a sense of spaciousness.

21’ Another high pitch exploration with a single pair.

25’ Base tone + hight pitch. Successful natural harmonic. Falldown and end.
